Internet Threats

Malware stands for malicious software programs designed to infiltrate or damage computer systems without the user's informed consent.

Common Types of Internet Threats

Worm

A malicious program that transfers from one computer to another by any type of means. Often it used a computer network to spread itself.

ILOVEYOU

It is a type of worm/virus created by a Filipino that was sent on email with a subject line "ILOVEYOU" and an attachment. According to Awati (2021), on May 4, 2000, the ILOVEYOU virus spread to an estimated 45 million users in just 10 days and caused about 10 billion in damages.

Trojan

A malicious program that is disguised as a useful program, but once downloaded or installed, leaves your PC unprotected and allows hackers to get your information.

Rogue Security Software

Tricks the user into supposing/believing that it is a security software.

Spyware

A program that runs in the background without you knowing it (thus called "spy"). It has the ability to monitor what you are currently doing and typing through keylogging.

Keyloggers

Used to record the keystroke done by the users. This is done to steal their password or any other sensitive information.

Adware

A program designed to send you advertisements, mostly as pop-ups.

Spam

Unwanted email mostly from bots or advertisers.

Phishing

Its goal is to acquire sensitive personal information like passwords and credit card details.

Pharming

A more complicated way of phishing where it exploits the DNS (Domain Name Service) system.
